A few things to know

Clear information. Thoughtful care.

Who can read my report?

Case staff and administrators with authorized access can read report details. Reviewers receive only report references, dates, and potential shared wording counts.

Is the report anonymous?

No. You must sign in, and each report is connected to your account so you can follow its progress. You can choose not to provide additional contact details to case staff.

When should I expect a response?

Response times depend on the institution’s staffing and procedures. This portal does not promise a response time and is not monitored continuously.

What if someone is in immediate danger?

Do not wait for a portal response. Contact your local emergency service or a verified campus emergency contact.
